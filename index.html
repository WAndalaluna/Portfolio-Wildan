About Me

A brief introduction to my background, focus areas, and working philosophy.

I am Wildan Rahmantya Andalaluna, an Informatics Engineering undergraduate at Universitas Padjadjaran focused on machine learning, product usability, and applied software design. I enjoy transforming ambiguous ideas into clear, testable, and maintainable solutions.

My mindset: curiosity paired with execution. I value collaboration, measurable impact, and continuous improvement across code quality, accessibility, and user experience.
